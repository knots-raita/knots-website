Services

We focus on classic air-cooled, carbureted, manual-transmission vehicles — servicing, engine overhauls and restoration for cars and motorcycles. If you don't see your vehicle here, ask us anyway.

What we specialize in

  • Air-cooled VW — Beetle, Bus, Karmann Ghia
  • Air-cooled, carbureted motorcycles — Ducati, BMW, Vespa, Lambretta, Yamaha SR400/500, trials and off-road bikes, Honda Super Cub (from mopeds to big bikes)
  • Suzuki Jimny (leaf-spring models only)

We also work on air-cooled Porsches (four-cylinder cars in-house; six-cylinder cars together with a partner shop) and the Toyota AE86. Most of what we handle is air-cooled, single- or twin-cylinder and carbureted; cars are mostly manual, but automatics are welcome.

What we do

  • Regular servicing and carburetor tuning
  • Suspension and electrical work
  • Engine overhauls
  • Body restoration
  • Everyday service: oil changes, inspections and general repairs

We look at the vehicle first and then discuss the work it needs, so we don't give quotes in advance.

Vehicle inspection (shaken)

We're preparing our official garage certification (our facilities have been inspected by the Okinawa Automobile Maintenance Promotion Association, and we're waiting for the association to file the application). Until it's approved we can't offer Japan's mandatory vehicle inspection, known as shaken. We'll post details here once we can.

How long it takes

As a rough guide: general servicing from 1 day, engine overhauls from 2 months, and full restorations from 1 year. We will tell you more once we have seen the vehicle.

Bringing your vehicle to us is free. If you would like us to collect it, that is a paid service. The same goes for handing it back: pick-up at the garage is free, and delivery to you is paid.

Other ways we can help

Insurance claim repairs

If you leave it to us, we handle the whole thing: dealing with your insurer, the repair and a loan car. Any make or model.

Selling your car or motorcycle

We buy vehicles outright and also sell on consignment. On consignment, we take a fee from the sale price that depends on how long we keep the vehicle. Where there is a market price, we appraise to it; for classics with no real market price, we appraise after seeing the vehicle in person. Outright purchase and consignment give different amounts.

Rental cars and bikes

We plan to rent out cars and a scooter, for sightseeing and as loan cars while yours is being repaired (insurance claims included): two Mira kei cars (automatic and manual), a Moco kei car and a 200 cc automatic Vespa. Booking is not open yet; we are preparing a dedicated site. Until then, message us on LINE.

Nationwide shipping

We ship cars, motorcycles, body shells and frames, high-end cars, parts and classics anywhere in Japan. Delivery only as far as a port (where you collect it) is also possible to suit your budget. Message us on LINE first.
