FAQ

The questions we hear most often.

What kinds of vehicles do you work on?

As a rule, we work on air-cooled, single- or twin-cylinder, carbureted machines, and on manual-transmission cars. Automatics are welcome too, and for anything else, just ask.

Can you work on air-cooled VWs?

Yes — they're a specialty. Contact us with your model and year.

Can you work on air-cooled Porsches?

We have experience with four-cylinder models. For six-cylinder models, we work with a partner air-cooled Porsche shop.

Can you overhaul engines?

Yes, it's one of our strengths, especially on air-cooled VWs. The scope depends on the vehicle and its condition, so please get in touch first.

Do you work on motorcycles?

Yes. Our focus is Ducati, BMW, Vespa, Lambretta, Yamaha SR, trials and off-road bikes and the Super Cub, from mopeds to big bikes.

Can you do vehicle inspections (shaken)?

Not yet. Our garage certification is still in preparation. We'll announce it once we can.

Can I bring a kei car, an automatic or an everyday car?

Yes. Whatever you drive, talk to us first and we'll tell you what we can take on.

How much will it cost?

We look at the vehicle first and then discuss the work it needs, so we can't give a quote in advance.
