FAQ

The questions we hear most often.

What kinds of vehicles do you work on?

As a rule, we work on air-cooled, single- or twin-cylinder, carbureted machines, and on manual-transmission cars. Automatics are welcome too, and for anything else, just ask.

Can you work on air-cooled VWs?

Yes — they're a specialty. Any model or year is fine. Contact us with the details.

Can you work on air-cooled Porsches?

We have experience with four-cylinder models. For six-cylinder models, we work with a partner air-cooled Porsche shop.

Do you work on the Suzuki Jimny?

Yes, leaf-spring models (such as the SJ30 and JA11). We only take leaf-spring Jimnys.

Can you overhaul engines?

Yes, it's one of our strengths, especially on air-cooled VWs. The scope depends on the vehicle and its condition, so please get in touch first.

Do you work on motorcycles?

Yes. Our focus is Ducati, BMW, Vespa, Lambretta, Yamaha SR, trials and off-road bikes and the Super Cub, from mopeds to big bikes. We prefer models from 2000 or earlier, and we do not take Harley-Davidsons.

Can you do vehicle inspections (shaken)?

Not yet. Our garage certification is still in preparation. We'll announce it once we can.

Can I bring a kei car, an automatic or an everyday car?

Yes. Whatever you drive, talk to us first and we'll tell you what we can take on.

How much will it cost?

We look at the vehicle first and then discuss the work it needs, so we can't give a quote in advance.

How long will my vehicle be with you?

As a rough guide: general servicing from 1 day, engine overhauls from 2 months, and full restorations from 1 year. We will tell you more once we have seen the vehicle.

Can I bring my vehicle in, or can you collect it?

Bringing it to us is free. If you would like us to collect it, that is a paid service. The same goes for handing it back: pick-up at the garage is free, and delivery to you is paid.

Can you handle an insurance claim repair?

Yes. If you leave it to us, we handle the whole thing: dealing with your insurer, the repair and a loan car. Any make or model.

I want to sell my car or motorcycle.

We buy vehicles outright and also sell on consignment. On consignment, we take a fee from the sale price that depends on how long we keep the vehicle. Where there is a market price, we appraise to it; for classics with no real market price, we appraise after seeing the vehicle in person.

Can you ship vehicles or parts across Japan?

Yes: cars, motorcycles, body shells and frames, high-end cars, parts and classics. Delivery only as far as a port is also possible to suit your budget. Message us on LINE first.

Do you rent out cars or bikes?

We plan to, for sightseeing and as loan cars while yours is being repaired. Booking is not open yet; we are preparing a dedicated site. Until then, message us on LINE.
