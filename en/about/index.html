About

Knots is run by an owner trained in mechanical engineering. Alongside everyday service, we source enthusiast cars and motorcycles, restore them and sell them.

The owner

Background

Grew up near the air-cooled VW specialist FLAT-4, which sparked a lifelong love of VWs.

Drag-raced a 1967 VW Beetle as a student, blowing up several self-tuned engines along the way — the foundation of the skills used today.

Took part in the Asia Cross Country Rally 2026 as a mechanic.
